About this network

A small research seismic network operated in the El Paso, Texas region, combining Raspberry Shake community seismometers with the broadband station KIDD (UTEP). Events are detected automatically with machine learning (PhaseNet), associated (GaMMA), located (NonLinLoc), and manually reviewed before appearing here. Most detections are quarry blasts from mining operations around the Franklin Mountains and Juárez; the catalog also contains locally recorded earthquakes.
